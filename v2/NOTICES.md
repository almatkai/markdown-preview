Its exact README, AUTHORS and manifest declarations are retained under `licenses/rust/r-efi-5.3.0`; choose MIT. It is a locked optional UEFI dependency, not compiled for the macOS/Windows targets tested here. Its missing full text remains an upstream packaging evidence gap, not invented legal clearance. No additional non-MIT data or icon license was found at the original extension path. The pinned beta repository root license supplies the beta fixture terms; no external contributor audit or legal clearance is claimed.

Npm validation dependencies are installed only from lockfiles inside disposable/node_modules directories and excluded from the Cargo deliverable. Their package-lock declarations can be inspected; shipped library fixture terms above are included in the aggregate. Do not ship installed node_modules as extension runtime. All source/license/asset fixture hashes are recorded in PROVENANCE.json and verified by `scripts/schema-and-integrity.cjs`.
